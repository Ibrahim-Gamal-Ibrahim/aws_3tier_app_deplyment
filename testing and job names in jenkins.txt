release the hook lifecycle:

for backend :
aws autoscaling complete-lifecycle-action \
  --lifecycle-hook-name backend-configuration-hook \
  --auto-scaling-group-name backend-asg \
  --lifecycle-action-result CONTINUE \
  --instance-id <INSTANCE_ID> \
  --region eu-north-1

for frontend:
aws autoscaling complete-lifecycle-action \
  --lifecycle-hook-name frontend-configuration-hook \
  --auto-scaling-group-name frontend-asg \
  --lifecycle-action-result CONTINUE \
  --instance-id <INSTANCE_ID> \
  --region eu-north-1

Lifecycle hook
= delays entering InService until configuration is ready

health_check_grace_period
= once it is entering/entered service, gives it time before ASG reacts to health-check failures

In your project, the hook is the main protection during configuration, while the grace period is an extra safety buffer against premature replacement.
